Debby is a project meant to facilitate independent debate practice. Instead of needing another team to practice debate, you can practice against Debby AI! Currently, the forms of debate are restricted to Parliamentary and MSPDP, but more debate types are coming soon! After selecting Parliamentary debate, you can further select topics by tournament. When preparing for NPDI, you can select the NPDI option and only get topics from previous NPDI tournaments. After given a topic, a customizable timer will allow you to set the time of your speech. Then select "Start Recording" and start affirming the topic! Before your timer expires, submit your speech and wait for a response. DebbyAI will respond with a written speech of its own. Then, you will be given a rebuttal speech to counter Debby's speech and defend your own points. After submitting the rebuttal, a decision will be made on the winner. The AI will respond with an RFD (Reason for Decision) as well as feedback tips to improve. Additionally, a WPM graph will show up which shows how fast you spoke at 5 second intervals. Ideally, this is as close to a straight line as possible to signify a constant pace while speaking. Additionally, after each round (Your speech, Debby's speech, your rebuttal, and feedback), all the speech transcripts, feedback, and speech statistics are saved in a database. You can click the "View Entries" to look at previous rounds and go over past feedback. 

Another feature of this webpage is the Case GPT. You can enter any topic and select which side (Affirmation or Negation). The AI will create a correctly formatted debate case with multiple contentions and pieces of evidence for whatever topic it is assigned. Currently, the cases are either MSPDP or Parliamentary format, with more types coming soon!
